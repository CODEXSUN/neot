# Business Concepts

## Core model

- Outcome: the result the business wants.
- Customer: the person or group that receives value.
- Process: the ordered work that produces an outcome.
- Owner: the person accountable for the process.
- Trigger: the event that starts work.
- Input: the data or material required to start.
- Output: the result produced by the process.
- Rule: a condition that controls a decision.
- Exception: a case that cannot follow the standard flow.
- Control: a check that reduces risk.
- Metric: a measure used to evaluate the result.

## Common domains

- Sales: lead, opportunity, quote, order, conversion, pipeline.
- Service: request, ticket, priority, response time, resolution, satisfaction.
- Operations: capacity, queue, cycle time, handoff, backlog, quality.
- Finance: revenue, cost, margin, cash flow, invoice, payment, variance.
- Inventory: item, stock, reorder point, lead time, movement, shrinkage.
- Projects: scope, milestone, dependency, risk, issue, change, acceptance.

## Measures

Use volume, time, cost, quality, conversion, reliability, and customer impact. Ask for the current
value, target value, measurement period, and data source.
