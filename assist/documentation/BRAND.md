# NEOT Brand

## Brand idea

NEOT means **Next Era. Own Tomorrow.**

The name gives learners a clear promise. Education prepares students for the next era. Students own
their progress and build their tomorrow with support from masters and organisations.

## Tagline

**Learn today. Own tomorrow.**

Use this tagline on product, login, and organisation-facing surfaces. Use the expanded name meaning
when the page needs more context.

## Logo concept: NEOT Portal

The name-led symbol uses one continuous line to form an `N`. Two open portal corners surround the
letter. The upper-left opening represents today. The lower-right opening represents tomorrow. The
rising diagonal connects both moments and shows forward progress.

The symbol deliberately avoids books, graduation caps, pencils, people, and institutional crests. It
can represent learning without limiting NEOT to a conventional school identity.

## Logo rules

- Use one colour for the complete mark.
- Use the black mark on light surfaces.
- Use the white mark on dark surfaces.
- Keep clear space equal to the width of one portal corner.
- Do not add gradients, shadows, outlines, or extra symbols.
- Do not place text inside the mark.
- Do not stretch or rotate the mark.

The master SVG uses a `64 × 64` view box. It remains clear at favicon and application-icon sizes.

## Voice

NEOT speaks with care and clarity. Use short sentences. Describe the next learning action. Avoid
competitive rank language when welfare or development evidence gives a better measure.
